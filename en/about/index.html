Questions people often ask about the artist and his work.

Who is Lee Yunsung?

Lee Yunsung (b. 1985) is a Korean painter who lives and works in Korea. He received a B.F.A. in Western Painting from Chung-Ang University in 2011, won the DOOSAN Yonkang Arts Award in Visual Arts in 2014, and took part in the DOOSAN Residency New York in 2016.

What is his work about?

He reworks the iconography of classical painting and sculpture — the Torso, Danae, Laocoon, Medusa, the Annunciation and the Zodiac — in the visual language of manga, anime and pixels, bringing Western classical images together with East Asian comics and animation. He works mainly in oil, and also makes sculpture editions, pen-plotter drawings and digital works.

What are his main series?

Torso, Danae, The Annunciation, Laocoon, Medusa, Zodiac and SD Zodiac, The Last Judgment, and Prometheus and The Three Graces. All series can be seen under WORKS.

How is his name written?

His name is written Lee Yunsung, with the family name first (이윤성 in Korean). It sometimes appears as Yunsung Lee or Yun Sung Lee. He is a painter born in 1985, not to be confused with other people of the same name, such as photographers or writers.
